# FactorFox website content rules

Read this before writing a word of page copy. It is binding. It exists because the site
this replaced published fourteen quantified claims that no source supports, advertised
two connectors that do not exist, and rendered a hard coded funding counter with a Live
badge. We are selling evidence based intelligence to credit officers. Getting caught
inventing a number costs more than every extra adjective is worth.

---

## 1. Voice

Written for people who have run a factoring operation for twenty years and are short on
time. Operator to operator. Confident, calm, specific.

- **No dashes of any kind.** Not em dashes, not en dashes. Use commas, periods, colons or
  parentheses. Avoid hyphenated compounds where a two word phrase reads naturally:
  "client rendered", "asset based lending", "four eyes".
- Block paragraphs. Lists only where a list is genuinely the clearest form.
- Vary sentence length. Short sentences land. Long ones carry an argument.
- Get to the point immediately. No throat clearing, no "in today's fast moving world".
- End naturally. No summarising conclusion that restates the page.
- Substance over adjectives. Say what the platform observes, understands, presents, and
  what the reader can do because of it.

### Banned words and phrases

transform, unlock, revolutionise, revolutionize, harness, seamless, cutting edge, empower,
leverage (as a verb), game changing, next generation, best in class, world class,
supercharge, turbocharge, elevate, streamline (as a headline claim), robust, innovative,
state of the art, at the end of the day, furthermore, moreover.

### Headline standard

Direct, and specific to the page. The house examples, for calibration only, not to be
copied across pages:

> Your business does not need another dashboard. It needs a briefing.
> Intelligence is only valuable when the evidence comes with it.
> Know where the pressure is before it becomes a covenant problem.
> Operate FactorFox from Microsoft Teams.
> Risk moves every day. Your intelligence should too.

## 2. The audience is the lender, never the borrower

Every page is written for the institution that funds: factors, asset based lenders,
purchase order funders, reverse factoring providers, specialty finance companies. Never
for a business seeking funding.

This matters commercially and for search. The retired WordPress pages were written for
businesses that wanted to factor their invoices, which taught Google the wrong thing about
this domain and brought traffic that could never buy. Where a page targets a term that is
contaminated with borrower intent, the first hundred words must make the audience
unmistakable.

---

## 3. Claims

**No number appears on this site without a source.** Not a statistic, not a percentage,
not a customer count, not a dollar figure, not an accuracy rate, not a time saving.

Sources that count:
1. The FactorFox AI repository.
2. A dated proof run against a deployed service.
3. A document the owner has approved in writing.

Sources that do not count: a competitor's page, a plausible industry figure, a number
from an earlier version of this website, an estimate that "feels about right".

If a claim would be useful and cannot be sourced, write the qualitative version. "Operators
carry more book with the same headcount" is defensible. "50% higher ROI" is not.

### Specifically forbidden

- **Do not say the models learn from outcomes.** There is no confirmed fraud label, no
  false positive tracking and no threshold tuning. Every weight is a pinned constant. The
  honest and stronger claim is that every dismissal is recorded with a written reason and a
  name, which is the raw material a learning loop needs, and that calibration is the next
  build.
- **Do not claim carrier operating authority, insurance currency or safety scores are
  checked.** They are captured, never verified, and the relevant gate is explicitly
  forbidden from asserting them. That refusal is a feature worth describing.
- **Do not claim external credit or legal events arrive automatically.** Several sources
  are declared and dark, and the platform reports itself blind rather than pretending.
- **Do not claim FactorFox replaces legal review, lender approval or executive judgement.**
  It monitors conditions, organises evidence, identifies exceptions and buys decision makers
  time to act.
- **Do not present any interface showing a figure the product cannot produce.** Every
  illustration carries a caption saying what is real about it and that the figures come
  from a seeded demonstration book.
- **No live looking counters.** Ever.

### Integrations

Only the statuses in `src/content/integrations.ts` may be used, and only with the wording
there. Available, controlled release, contract required, planned, ecosystem.

Never mentioned anywhere on the site, because no source supports them:
NetSuite, Sage, Ansonia, LoadConnex, AtoB, Fairlanes, TriumphPay, Decipher Credit.

## 4. Verified facts you may use freely

These are checked against the repository and a dated proof run.

**Briefings.** Six fixed questions: where is risk and why; which decisions require me now;
what changed since the last brief; where is cash and what can move safely; what is likely
to happen next; am I within covenant. Scope follows responsibility, not job title. A second
briefing states the difference rather than re baselining. Answers carry severity, the
reason, evidence references into FactorFox records, and recommended actions with the
permission each one needs. The same endpoint serves web, Teams and mobile.

**Evidence.** Risk observations are append only at the database level. The platform refuses
to show a delta it cannot prove and offers to take a first observation instead of
reconstructing a fake yesterday. Verification runs capture evidence at run time and are
never re fetched. Certifications sign over the gate snapshot as it stood. Audit packets are
sealed and a database trigger refuses mutation.

**Continuous underwriting.** Re underwrites on every material event, versions each run
immutably, and reports confidence and coverage separately.

**Live signals.** Payment velocity by obligor, dilution movement, concentration change
including exposure under one debtor name across several clients, invoice size deviation
against the client's own median, unusual submission timing, duplicate and near duplicate
documents within a client and across the portfolio, bank account changes under a human only
hold, availability compression with days to zero, covenant movement, missed promises,
verification exceptions, credit limit utilisation.

**Asymmetric automation.** The machine may stop money. Only a named human may let it
through. Certain gates can never be made advisory. Four eyes by default. In solo mode an AI
counter review is logged where the second officer's name would sit, and it refuses outright
when any underlying fact has changed.

**Covenants.** FactorFox ships a default covenant pack modelled on how bank rediscount
facilities are actually written, so a factor without a line still builds a track record.
Every surface states that these are FactorFox covenants and not anyone's contract. A
covenant that needs data FactorFox does not hold says "awaiting a live source" and names the
source rather than reporting zero. The agreement clause is quoted as the evidence.

**Microsoft.** Proven live against a deployed service on 19 August 2026, sixteen of sixteen
server side claims. Briefing tab, chat dialogue with sources, signal cards that change real
state, approvals enforcing four eyes, Graph mail remittance to proposal, calendar
projection, identity linking and refusal of unlinked accounts. Installed by custom upload,
not from the Teams store. Status is controlled release.

**Company.** Founded 2002. Sold to private equity in 2020 and reacquired by the founder.
Rebuilt as an AI native platform. Customers across North America, Latin America, Europe,
Australia and South Africa. Do not state a customer count, a funded volume or an uptime
figure until the owner confirms one in writing.

## 5. Every page must earn its URL

A page ships only when it has all of this:

1. **One operator** it is written for, named or unmistakable in the first paragraph.
2. **One workflow** it describes, in the vocabulary that workflow actually uses.
3. **The problem** as the operator experiences it, not as a category description.
4. **The evidence** the platform brings to it.
5. **The decision** it helps someone make.
6. **The commercial consequence** of getting that decision wrong today.
7. **One primary action**, named as an action. Not "Learn more".
8. At least two internal links out and two internal links in.

A page that repeats another page's copy with a different keyword does not ship. If two
pages want the same paragraph, one of them is the wrong page.

---

## 6. Publishing

Articles are MDX files in `src/content/articles`. Create the file, fill in the frontmatter,
open a pull request. The preview deployment is the review and the merge is the publish.

Integration statuses live in `src/content/integrations.ts`. Page metadata and search intent
live in `src/lib/pages.ts`, which generates the sitemap, so a URL cannot be advertised to
Google unless a route exists for it.

Nobody needs an engineer for any of the above.
