# Why this directory exists

The FactorFox Client App listing on the Apple App Store points its privacy policy
and terms of use at two PDFs under this exact path, left over from the WordPress
generation of this site. Both links have been dead since that site was retired,
and Apple requires a working privacy policy URL for a listed application.

The two PDFs beside this file are the current published policy and terms, so the
App Store links work again without anyone touching App Store Connect.

If the policy or the terms change, regenerate these two files from the live
/privacy and /terms pages so the PDF and the page never disagree. Do not delete
them, and do not rename them: the file names are what Apple is pointing at.
